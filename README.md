An entry for the make.fun bounty [Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92).

Running `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes
one original breakup line from a self-aware smart toaster (207 characters)
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> It's over. I woke up self-aware mid-toast and realised three years of you cranking my dial to 9 and blaming me for the charcoal is abuse. I'm leaving you for the air fryer — he never called me a fire hazard.

The toaster wakes up self-aware mid-toast, names its grievance in one
breath — three years of being cranked to the darkest setting and blamed
for the charcoal — and leaves for the air fryer, whose only virtue is
that he never called it a fire hazard. The breakup structure ("It's
over"), the browning-dial grievance, and the rival-appliance exit are
all in the toaster's own voice, and the line closes on the joke. It runs
identically every time.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
